We carry both! New records are sealed and come straight from the distributor or label. Used records are carefully inspected and graded so you always know exactly what condition you’re getting.

We follow the Goldmine grading standard. Mint (M) means perfect and unplayed, Near Mint (NM) shows no obvious wear, Very Good Plus (VG+) has light surface marks that don’t affect play, and Very Good (VG) has noticeable wear but still plays well. Every used record is labeled with both the vinyl and sleeve grade.
